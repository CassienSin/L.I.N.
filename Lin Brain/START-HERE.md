# Lin Brain — getting started

This is a setup guide, not a personal or business fact about you.

Place UTF-8 .txt or .md notes in this folder. Lin reads these files on your computer.
Choose Search my brain in the workspace, then ask about a specific name or phrase.
Matching passages show their filename and line numbers. You can inspect the passage.

The first version searches keywords. PDF, Word, images, and audio files are not indexed.
Use the Memory panel to explicitly save a fact, correct it later, or forget it.
Disconnecting this folder removes its passages from search without deleting your files.
